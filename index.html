    New here? Here's how your garden grows.
    1. Add an intention, choose a plant, and pick a focus duration.
    2. Start the timer. Your seed becomes a sprout, a young plant, and then a mature plant.
    3. Finish the session to earn one plant. Pauses are welcome; resetting earns no plant.
    4. Take an optional five-minute break, then begin again when you're ready.

    The 10-second demo is just a preview and never changes your garden or statistics. Your data stays in this browser; no account or upload is needed. Use one active tab.
